# What Subsidies Can Chinese Business Owners Operating Online Stores and E-Commerce in Japan Apply For? The Ultimate Guide

According to Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry data for fiscal 2023, Japan's e-commerce market has reached 22.7 trillion yen, with year-on-year growth of 4.0%, and cross-border e-commerce growing at a high rate of 12.3%—which sufficiently explains why the government continues to increase support for the e-commerce industry. **In 2024, Japan's Small and Medium Enterprise Agency and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry allocated a subsidy budget exceeding 50 billion yen for e-commerce and digital transformation, covering the entire chain from storefront renovation, marketing, logistics, and system development**. As a Chinese business owner in Japan, if you are currently operating or planning to launch cross-border e-commerce or domestic Japanese online stores, this guide will help you systematically understand which subsidies you can apply for, how much each offers, and how to plan your application timeline—allowing subsidies to become a powerful support for your expansion costs.

## Japan's E-Commerce Subsidy Policy Landscape: Three Major Systems and Government Annual Budget Scale

Japan's government subsidy system for e-commerce enterprises is broadly divided into **three tiers**: national level (Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry / Small and Medium Enterprise Agency), prefectural level, and municipal level. For fiscal 2024, the total national-level budget allocated to e-commerce transformation subsidies is as follows:

- **事業再構築補助金 (Business Restructuring Subsidy)**: Annual budget approximately 1,500–2,000 billion yen, with e-commerce and digital transformation category accounting for approximately 15–20%, with actual portions applicable to online stores approximately 250–300 billion yen
- **小規模事業者持続化補助金 (Small Business Sustainability Grant)**: Annual budget approximately 26 billion yen, with e-commerce improvement as the priority adoption direction
- **ものづくり・商社・サービス補助金 (Monozukuri Subsidy)**: Annual budget approximately 90 billion yen, with cross-border e-commerce and overseas sales function enhancement as priority support areas

This means **the government's total annual allocation to e-commerce-related subsidies exceeds 5 billion yen**. Clearly, if your online store and warehouse management system requires investment upgrades and marketing tools, the government almost certainly has a corresponding subsidy—the key is recognizing which one fits your situation.

## 小規模事業者持続化補助金 (Small Business Sustainability Grant): The Easiest-to-Access Online Store Subsidy

### Subsidy Overview and Amount

The Small Business Sustainability Grant (hereinafter 「Small Business Sustainability Subsidy」) **is the most suitable subsidy for startup online stores, solo entrepreneurs, or small team business owners**. Managed by the Japan Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the National Federation of Small Business Associations, it holds two recruitment rounds annually (typically spring and autumn).

**Subsidy Amount and Subsidy Rate:**
- Subsidy rate: **2/3 of eligible expenses (approximately 66.7%)**
- Maximum subsidy amount: **500,000 yen** (approximately 42,000 yen per month in subsidies, up to 500,000 yen annually)
- Eligible applicants: Small business operators with 5 or fewer employees (including the representative), **Chinese-owned or partnership enterprises are eligible as long as this condition is met**

**Calculation Example:** If you invest 750,000 yen total in online store redesign, product photography, and logistics box design, the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy can cover 500,000 yen of this amount at 66.7%, meaning you pay approximately 250,000 yen out of pocket while the subsidy covers approximately 500,000 yen.

### Eligible Expense Categories and Application Examples

The Small Business Sustainability Subsidy allows very flexible expense categories, **perfectly suited to the entire e-commerce operational cycle**:

| Expense Category | Specific Examples | Expected Amount Range |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| 広告・販売促進費 (Advertising / Sales Promotion) | SNS advertising, Google Shopping, email marketing tools | 20,000–100,000 yen monthly |
| 店舗改装費 (Store Renovation) | Online store UI/UX design, page restructuring | 300,000–800,000 yen one-time |
| 機器・装置費 (Equipment / Devices) | Printers, photography lights, product management system software | 100,000–500,000 yen one-time |
| 旅費 (Travel) | Sourcing location visits, customer visits | 5,000–20,000 yen monthly |
| 外注費・委託費 (Outsourcing / Consignment) | Photographers, designers, translators, system development | 20,000–200,000 yen monthly |

**Real-World Case Study**: Ms. Li established a clothing cross-border e-commerce business and registered a Japanese corporation in January 2024, with 4 employees. She simultaneously submitted a 「Small Business Sustainability Subsidy」application in March, planning to invest in: website restructuring (500,000 yen) + product photography outsourcing (250,000 yen) + six months of Google advertising (300,000 yen) = total 1,050,000 yen. The Small Business Sustainability Subsidy approval rate is approximately 70–75% (per official statistical data). She received 500,000 yen in subsidy in July, paying approximately 550,000 yen out of pocket, effectively cutting her online store startup costs in half. This funding allowed her to accelerate hiring a video editor and customer service staff starting in August, with monthly online store revenue growing to 3 million yen, creating a positive feedback loop.

### Application Eligibility and Process

**Eligibility Requirements (all must be met):**
- 5 or fewer employees (representative counts as 1)
- Continuous business operation for at least the past 1 year (must submit previous fiscal year tax return proof)
- **Japanese tax registration (corporate tax or individual business tax number) is required**; Chinese nationals must have valid residence status and be able to open a Japanese bank account
- Must be able to cooperate with follow-up surveys for 1 year after subsidy project completion (follow-up cooperation required within 1 year after application)

**Application Process and Timeline:**

1. **募集発表 (Recruitment Announcement)**: Each recruitment round is typically announced 2–3 months in advance (spring round typically announced in February, autumn round typically announced in August)
2. **受付期間 (Application Period)**: Approximately 30–45 days for application submission window
3. **申请提出 (Application Submission)**: Submit application form, business plan (approximately 1–2 A4 pages), financial statements, and ID copy to your local Chamber of Commerce or Business Association
4. **審査期間 (Review Period)**: 3–4 months
5. **採択結果公表 (Adoption Results Announcement)**: Approximately 4–5 months after application
6. **事業実施 (Project Implementation)**: Complete expenditures within 6–12 months after adoption
7. **実績報告・補助金交付 (Performance Report / Subsidy Disbursement)**: After project completion, submit receipts and completion report, then subsidy disbursement occurs after 1–2 months of review

**Expected Timeline Example**:
- Mid-March application submission → Mid-July adoption notification → Before mid-March 2025 complete procurement and payment → April 2025 submit performance report → Around June 2025 subsidy arrives

**Single subsidy cycle is approximately 12–15 months**, so 「applying now and receiving money next year」is the norm.

## 事業再構築補助金 (Business Restructuring Subsidy): The Gold Mine for Mid-to-Large Online Stores and Digital Transformation Leaps

### Subsidy Overview: Why This Is the 「Largest Cup」

The Business Restructuring Subsidy (commonly abbreviated as 「Business Restructuring Subsidy」) **is one of the largest subsidy categories in Japan**, primarily targeting:
- Businesses that experienced sales declines during the pandemic and need to restructure their business model
- Transition from offline to online (e-commerce)
- Expansion from domestic to cross-border markets

**Subsidy Amount and Subsidy Rate:**
- Maximum subsidy: **Standard type 15 million yen, large-scale loss-making business type 30 million yen**
- Subsidy rate: **2/3 (66.7%) for SMEs, 3/4 (75%) for small businesses**
- Expected out-of-pocket: For a 20 million yen project application, SMEs need to pay approximately 6.67 million yen out of pocket, with subsidy covering 13.33 million yen

**This means**: If your online store investment includes warehouse management system development, cross-border logistics system construction, brand rebranding, and personnel expansion, with total investment potentially reaching 15–30 million yen, the government can subsidize up to **20 million yen or more**.

### Eligible Expenses and Support Scope

Business Restructuring Subsidy expense categories are far broader than the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy, suitable for 「large-scale expansion」enterprises:

**建設費・改装費 (Construction / Renovation)**: Building or renovating e-commerce warehouses, offices, potentially millions of yen
**機器・装置費 (Equipment / Devices)**: ERP systems, automated warehouse equipment, WMS, CRM software, 3PL integration systems
**システム構築費 (System Development)**: Complete website redevelopment, multilingual versions, payment system integration, inventory management backend
**外注費・委託費 (Outsourcing / Consignment)**: Custom development, UI/UX design, brand strategy, marketing agencies, logistics agencies
**広告宣伝費 (Advertising)**: Television advertising, large portal website advertising, SEO/SEM, KOL partnerships, trade show participation
**人件費 (Personnel Costs)**: Temporary employee costs (under specific conditions), training expenses

**Key Data**: According to fiscal 2023 Business Restructuring Subsidy statistics, the e-commerce and digital transformation category has an **adoption rate of approximately 50–55%** (relatively high), with average subsidy amount approximately 8.9 million yen.

### Application Eligibility and Key Conditions

Business Restructuring Subsidy review standards are far stricter than the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy, due to larger amounts and clear policy objectives (encouraging 「true restructuring」rather than minor improvements).

**Eligibility Requirements (key items):**
- **Sales Decline Requirement**: During any month in the past 6 months, monthly sales must be 20% or more below the same month in the previous year (although the title is 「restructuring,」most cases still require proof of past pandemic or market impacts)
  - **For Chinese enterprises that are newly established or lack previous year data, alternative application reasons such as 「entering new business fields」can substitute**
- **Business Scale**: 5 or more employees is the standard (small businesses can also apply, but with weaker competitive position)
- **認定支援機関 (Certified Support Agency)**: Must obtain a support confirmation letter from a government-recognized support agency such as a Zeirishi (tax accountant), Chamber of Commerce, or SME Management Consultant before application (this is a hard requirement; you cannot apply directly yourself)

**This point is most likely to become an obstacle for Chinese businesses**: You need to find a recognized support agency willing to support you and issue a support confirmation letter. Many Chambers of Commerce and tax accountants have reservations about Chinese enterprises (language, tax compliance concerns), so **identifying support agencies 3–4 months in advance is critical**.

### Application Process and Review Cycle

1. **募集期間 (Recruitment Period)**: Typically 2–3 recruitment rounds per year (spring, autumn, winter), each with application window of approximately 30–45 days
2. **事業計画書作成 (Business Plan Development)**: This is the core requirement. Must prepare 15–20 page detailed business plan including:
   - 現状分析 (Current situation analysis—market, competition, company challenges)
   - 再構築戦略 (Restructuring strategy—why choose this path, market prospects)
   - 3年間の経営シミュレーション (3-year management simulation—revenue/profit forecasts, ROI calculations)
   - 資金調達計画 (Financing plan—combination of loans + subsidies)
3. **支援機関を通じた提出 (Submission Through Support Agency)**: Must be submitted by or accompanied by a Certified Support Agency
4. **書類審査 → プレゼンテーション面接 (Document Review → Presentation Interview)**: Applicants passing initial review must participate in online/in-person presentation (5–10 minutes explaining plan, Q&A)
5. **採択結果通知 (Adoption Notification)**: Generally approximately 2–3 months after application
6. **事業実施期間 (Project Implementation Period)**: Complete expenditures within 12–24 months after adoption (longer than Small Business Sustainability Subsidy, favorable for large projects)
7. **実績報告 (Performance Report)**: After completion, submit receipts and achievement report, review period approximately 1–2 months

**Total Cycle**: Recruitment announcement → Application → Presentation → Adoption → 12–24 months implementation → Subsidy disbursement, **full process typically requires 18–30 months**.

## ものづくり・商社・サービス補助金 (Monozukuri Subsidy): Dedicated Channel for Cross-Border E-Commerce and Overseas Sales

### Why This Subsidy Is Particularly Advantageous for Cross-Border E-Commerce

The Monozukuri Subsidy (hereinafter 「Monozukuri Subsidy」) **while nominally designed for manufacturing, trading, and service enterprises, explicitly lists cross-border e-commerce and overseas sales (external exports) as priority support areas**.

If your online store primarily sells Japanese products to China, Taiwan, and Southeast Asia, **this subsidy is the best match**.

**Subsidy Amount:**
- Standard type: **7.5 million to 12.5 million yen**
- Large-scale project type (significant equipment introduction): maximum **30 million yen**
- Subsidy rate: **1/2 (50%)**
- Expected out-of-pocket: For a 10 million yen project application, pay 5 million yen out of pocket, with subsidy covering 5 million yen

**Fiscal 2024 Data**: Monozukuri Subsidy had approximately 4,500 adoption cases, with digital transformation and overseas expansion category accounting for approximately 30%, meaning approximately 1,350 cases received subsidies, with average subsidy amount approximately 8.2 million yen.

### Eligible Expenses (Cross-Border E-Commerce Specific Examples)

| Expense Category | Specific Examples (Cross-Border E-Commerce Operations) | Expected Unit Price Range |
|---------|------------------|-----------|
| 機械・装置費 (Equipment / Devices) | Multilingual customer support system, live commerce streaming equipment | 2–5 million yen |
| システム構築費 (System Development) | Cross-border e-commerce platform integration (Amazon, Shopee, Lazada compatible), international payment system | 3–8 million yen |
| 外注費 (Outsourcing) | Overseas SEO measures, multilingual content creation, international logistics agency contracts | 1–4 million yen |
| 広告宣伝費 (Advertising) | TikTok Shop advertising, YouTube multilingual campaigns, influencer cooperation | 2–6 million yen |
| 研修費用 (Training Expenses) | Overseas marketing training, language training (for employees) | 500,000 to 2 million yen |
| 出張費 (Travel Expenses) | Target market sales agent development visits, local trade show participation, business trip expenses | 500,000 to 1.5 million yen |

### Application Eligibility and Key Conditions

**Eligibility Requirements (from cross-border e-commerce enterprise perspective):**
- **Creating 「new added value」in business**: Examples include 「launching new multilingual Chinese/English website」「entering Lazada/Shopee」「implementing live commerce」, etc., representing clearly different initiatives from existing business
- **Number of Employees**: 3 or more is standard (manufacturing 1 or more, retail/service 5 or more, business operation for 3+ years since corporate establishment is preferable)
- **Receiving Support from Certified Support Agency**: Similarly, prior support confirmation is mandatory

**Greatest Advantage**: Eligible expense categories are extremely broad, from 「warehouse renovation」to 「TikTok advertising」can be applied for in a bundle, **very suitable for enterprises planning a one-time 「systematic cross-border upgrade」**.

### Application Process and Timeline

1. **募集期間 (Recruitment Period)**: Typically 2–3 rounds per year (spring, autumn, winter)
2. **事業計画書要件 (Business Plan Requirements)**: 20–30 pages, including product planning document, overseas market analysis, competitive analysis, 3-year business plan, financing plan
3. **Submission Method**: Electronic application via jGrants (Japan government official subsidy application portal)
4. **審査プロセス (Review Process)**:
   - First Round: Document and business plan feasibility review
   - Second Round: Presentation and interviews
   - Third Round: Site investigation (applicable to/selected candidates)
5. **From Adoption to Implementation**: Complete project within 15–24 months after adoption
6. **実績報告 (Performance Report)**: After completion, submit financial report and performance report

**Expected Timeline**:
- March recruitment starts → End of April application deadline → June adoption results → June 2024 – September 2025 implementation → October 2025 performance report submission → December 2025 subsidy arrival

**Total cycle 18–21 months**, longer than Small Business Sustainability Subsidy, but with significantly larger subsidy amounts.

Beyond Japan's central government subsidies, **each prefecture and municipality also offers subsidies specifically for e-commerce and digitalization, typically 2–10 million yen, with relatively relaxed competition**.

**Tokyo Metropolis:**
- 「Small Business Digital Transformation Support Project」: 2/3 subsidy rate, 1 million yen maximum, 1–2 recruitment rounds annually

- 「SME Business Continuity and Business Transformation Support Project」: 2 million yen maximum, 50–70% subsidy rate, 1–2 recruitment rounds annually

**Other Local Governments:**
- Searchable via subsidy search sites 「J-Net21」or 「Subsidy Portal」

**Important Note**: If your company is registered in Tokyo, you can apply not only for national-level subsidies but also **simultaneously apply for Tokyo Metropolitan-level subsidies, with no conflicts and simultaneous application possible**. Expected subsidies from central + prefectural governments combined can reach **1.5–2 million yen annually**.

## Common Mistakes and Scoring Points in Applications

### Mistake 1: Thinking 「Subsidies」and 「Loans」are the same thing

**Misconception**: Receiving a subsidy also means you have to repay the money.

**Reality**: A subsidy (補助金) is 「government-provided non-repayable funds」; a loan (融資) requires periodic repayment. Subsidies do not need to be repaid, but have compliance usage and reporting obligations.

### Mistake 2: 「If I applied for a small business subsidy, I can't apply for a larger subsidy」

**Misconception**: A business cannot apply for multiple subsidies in the same year.

**Reality**: **Small Business Sustainability Grant + Monozukuri Subsidy + Prefectural Subsidy can be simultaneously applied for with absolutely no restrictions**. The key is ensuring each project's expense ranges do not overlap when applying. For example, if 「website restructuring 500,000 yen」is claimed in the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy application, it cannot be reduplicated in the Monozukuri Subsidy—but other parts of online store expansion (such as new system development 1 million yen) can be completely claimed in the Monozukuri Subsidy.

**Ms. Li's Case Study (Month 6 Application)**: She simultaneously submitted a Small Business Sustainability Subsidy application (website + photography + advertising totaling 1.05 million yen claimed, expecting 500,000 yen) and a prefectural subsidy application (staff training + data analysis system totaling 800,000 yen claimed), with the two subsidies' expenses completely non-overlapping; both were approved, receiving combined approximately 900,000 yen, representing approximately 42% of total investment.

### Mistake 3: 「Foreign individuals can also apply」

**Misconception**: Chinese people (including Chinese nationals, Taiwan, Hong Kong, etc.) can directly apply for subsidies.

**Reality**: Subsidy applicants must be **Japanese corporations or self-employed individuals with Japanese tax registration numbers and valid residence status**. Foreign individuals or companies cannot apply directly. If you haven't yet registered a corporation in Japan, **the first step is to apply for corporate establishment at the Legal Affairs Bureau (typically 1–2 weeks to complete), while simultaneously registering for corporate tax number at the Tax Office (approximately 1 week), with the entire process completable within 1 month**.

### Mistake 4: 「My Japanese isn't good; subsidy applications are too difficult」

**Reality**: Subsidy application documents do have some Japanese language threshold, particularly documents like 「business plans」that require logical thinking and industry understanding. **However, you don't need to complete it yourself—Certified Support Agencies (Zeirishi, Chambers of Commerce, Management Consultants) will help you draft**. Your responsibility is providing clear business information and financial data in Chinese; they handle translation and refinement into standardized Japanese applications.

### Scoring Point 1: 「Sustainability・ESG Compliance」

From 2024 onwards, government subsidy review increasingly emphasizes corporate ESG commitments (environment, labor, governance). If your online store is engaged in 「renewable energy use」「waste recycling」「increasing female management positions」etc., **you will receive obvious scoring boosts in Monozukuri Subsidy and Business Restructuring Subsidy reviews**.

### Scoring Point 2: 「Existing Overseas Sales Track Record」

In Monozukuri Subsidy and Business Restructuring Subsidy reviews, having existing overseas sales track records (past 12 months of overseas revenue) strengthens applications when clearly noted in the business plan. Even with small track record (monthly tens of thousands of yen level), a single phrase 「already have sales in 3 countries」dramatically increases credibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: I'm Chinese, and my company was established only half a year ago in Japan. Can I apply for subsidies?

**A:** Yes, but choices are limited. **The Small Business Sustainability Grant has no establishment time requirements**, requiring only 1 year of continuous business operation (meaning the company must be at least 1 year old). **The Business Restructuring Subsidy requires either 「pandemic period sales decline」or 「new business entry」, with no hard establishment time requirements, but needs Certified Support Agency assistance**. The Monozukuri Subsidy typically requires 3+ years of business operation with financial data comparison basis; startups have difficulty passing. **Recommendation: Immediately contact a Chamber of Commerce or Zeirishi after establishment, prioritize applying for Small Business Sustainability Subsidy within 9–12 months, while simultaneously preparing sufficient financial and market data for Monozukuri and Business Restructuring Subsidies**.

### Q2: When will I receive the subsidy money? I need funds urgently for expansion; will it take too long?

**A:** Subsidy disbursement cycles are indeed long. **Small Business Sustainability Grant: application → adoption → expenditure completion → performance report → subsidy disbursement, entire cycle approximately 12–15 months. Monozukuri and Business Restructuring Subsidies: even longer cycles, typically 18–24 months**. In other words, **if you apply in March this year, the fastest you'll receive money is next year's first half**. Therefore, subsidies cannot be treated as 「emergency financing」but rather as 「strategic funding」. If you need operational funds urgently now, **simultaneously apply for subsidies + apply to banks for 「unsecured financing」or 「subsidy-repayment-linked financing」(government-backed low-interest loans), use loans for operations first, and repay with subsidies when they arrive**. Many Japanese banks are willing to provide bridge financing to enterprises with 「already-adopted subsidies」under quite favorable terms.

### Q3: If my subsidy application is rejected (not adopted), can I reapply next round?

**A:** Yes, **rejected enterprises can reapply in the next recruitment round**. However, it's recommended to identify rejection reasons first. Common reasons include: (1) Business plan's market analysis insufficient, profitability model unclear (2) Enterprise's financial condition concerning (such as 3 years of losses) (3) Support agency's support opinion not strong enough. **If first application is rejected, invest 2–3 months deepening business plan, invite a consultant for free diagnosis (many Chambers of Commerce offer free diagnosis), then refine the application, next round adoption probability will clearly rise**. Statistics show that second applications after improvements have adoption rates of approximately 50–60%, far higher than initial applications' 35–40%.

### Q4: I operate both an online store and a physical retail location in Japan simultaneously. Can subsidies cover both?

**A:** **Yes, but must apply separately**. If you apply for 「Monozukuri Subsidy」or 「Business Restructuring Subsidy」, you can simultaneously list online store upgrades and physical store renovation as one integrated project—provided the business plan clearly explains how both cooperate (for example, 「directing offline foot traffic to online, showcasing online sales products offline」). **Small Business Sustainability Grant typically targets relatively single project types, usually concentrated in one main business direction**. Recommendation: If investment exceeds 1 million yen and involves multiple business points, prioritize Monozukuri or Business Restructuring Subsidies, with broader expense coverage scope.

### Q5: During the subsidy review process, can I modify application contents (for example, equipment purchase lists, budget amounts)?

**A:** **You typically cannot make changes during the review period before receiving the adoption notice**. However, **after adoption, during project implementation you can apply for 「modification approval」(change approval request)**, as long as modifications don't violate subsidy principles, don't affect the essential nature of the subsidy project, and stay within total approved budget, **modifications will generally be approved in most cases**. For example, website development budget overruns (changing from 2 million to 2.5 million yen), but total subsidy amount (for example, 1 million yen) remains unchanged, meaning enterprise's out-of-pocket increases, such modifications are generally approvable. However, if you want to temporarily change from 「online store to warehouse system」which represents essential change, approval will be difficult. **Recommendation: When applying, write equipment lists somewhat loosely, reserving 10–15% flexibility space**.

### Q6: After receiving the subsidy, how will the government monitor my usage? Will they audit accounts?

**A:** **Yes, and examination is very strict**. Subsidy management departments (such as SME Agency, Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry) conduct monitoring at the following stages:

1. **Receipt Examination During Performance Reports**: Must submit all purchase invoices, payment proofs, bank transfer records, ensuring funds actually used for subsidy target projects
2. **On-Site Inspection**: Subsidy organizations may dispatch personnel for site visits to verify online store operations, equipment deployment, ensuring funds actually invested
3. **Post-Implementation Evaluation**: For 1 year after subsidy completion, government tracks enterprise performance results (such as 「planned to increase monthly revenue by 1 million yen, what was actual achievement?」), collecting data for next fiscal year subsidy policy adjustments
4. **Special Audit**: If irregularities detected (such as fraudulent invoices, subsidy misappropriation), triggers criminal audit, consequences including subsidy full
