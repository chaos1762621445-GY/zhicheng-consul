## Core Overview of Automotive Service Facility Grants — Understanding True Characteristics to Avoid Pitfalls

The Japanese government's subsidies for the automotive repair and maintenance sector primarily consist of **three categories of systems**, each targeting different enterprise growth stages:

**1. Equipment Investment Tax Deduction and Subsidies Based on the SME Management Strengthening Law**
- Supervising Authority: Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry · Small and Medium Enterprise Agency
- Core Content: Purchasing or modifying automotive repair equipment (diagnostic machines, lifts, welding machines, etc.) qualifies for subsidy
- Subsidy Rate: **30–50%**; equipment investment of approximately **10 million yen** can yield **3–5 million yen**
- Target: Small and medium enterprises and individual proprietors with **20–300 employees**

**2. Monozukuri Subsidy (Manufacturing Industry Competitiveness Enhancement Subsidy)**
- Supervising Authority: Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry
- Core Content: Focuses on 「productivity enhancement」 「new product development」 「process improvement」
- Subsidy Rate: **1/2 to 2/3**; subsidy amounts **500,000 yen to 10 million yen** in phases
- Application Period: **2–3 public offerings annually** (typically April, August, December)

**3. Employment Adjustment Subsidy · Human Resource Recruitment and Development Support Grant**
- Supervising Authority: Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare
- Core Content: Subsidies for newspaper recruitment, training, salary increases, etc.
- Subsidy Amount: Monthly basis approximately **150,000–300,000 yen**; annually approximately **1.8–3.6 million yen**
- Application Period: Maximum 3 years (varies by industry)

**Special Attention for Chinese Proprietors**: These three systems **can be stacked for simultaneous application**, but you must avoid duplicate subsidization of the same project. For example, a single diagnostic machine cannot simultaneously claim both 「Equipment Investment Tax Deduction」 and 「Monozukuri Subsidy」 for the same equipment cost, but you can use 「Equipment Investment Tax Deduction」 for equipment and 「Human Resource Recruitment Support Grant」 for new employee training.

- **Countermeasure**: Execute address changes at municipal offices immediately; align residence card and tax office notifications simultaneously

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — Six Essential Questions Before Application

### Q1: Can Multiple Subsidies Be Simultaneously Applied For and Received? What Legal Risks Exist?

**A:** **Possible, but duplicate subsidy of identical expenses is absolutely prohibited.**
Example: Purchasing 1.5 million yen diagnostic machine:
- ❌ NG: Apply for 1.5 million yen subsidy under 「Monozukuri Subsidy」 **PLUS** claim identical 1.5 million yen under 「Equipment Investment Tax Deduction」 = Duplicate
- ✅ OK: Apply for 1 million yen under 「Monozukuri Subsidy」 **PLUS** 500,000 yen tax deduction for remainder = Division
- ✅ OK: 1.5 million yen diagnostic machine under 「Monozukuri Subsidy」 **PLUS** separate employee training under 「Human Resource Recruitment Grant」 = Different expense categories

When incorporating multiple applications into single submission, **always document 「concurrent subsidy status」** in designated field. Concealed multiple applications trigger post-adoption 「refund order plus penalty interest」 sanctions (per Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare guidance). Chinese proprietors tend to over-pursue 「multiple applications = greater profit」 logic, requiring **mandatory specialist consultation** before application.

### Q2: How Are Received Subsidies Treated in Tax Filing for That Year?

**A:** **Subsidies constitute 「taxable income」. They provide no tax reduction.**
Example: Receipt of 1 million yen subsidy:
- Accounting Treatment: 「Subsidy Revenue」 entered as 1 million yen taxable income
- Tax Treatment: 100 million yen counted toward profit in corporate/income tax calculation
- Consequence: Net profit unchanged; subsidy receipt may actually increase tax liability

**Exception exists**: 「Equipment Investment Tax Deduction System」 providing 「tax credit」 reduces actual tax owed. Combining both requires **tax professional calculation**. Widespread misunderstanding among Chinese proprietors that 「subsidy receipt = tax savings」 creates frequent filing complications. Consult tax advisor at application phase regarding 「subsidy accounting treatment」.

### Q3: What Level of Detail Should 「Business Plan」 Documents Contain?

**A:** **A4 paper 3–5 pages standard; include quantified targets plus qualitative objectives across 3-year span.**
Minimum structure:
1. **Current Situation Analysis** (1 page): Prior 3 years' revenue trends, competitive environment, recognized challenges
2. **Project Overview** (1 page): 「What equipment will be purchased」 「Why does this enhance competitiveness」 「Timeline and budget」
3. **Numerical Targets** (1 page): 「Following subsidy project completion, ◎ year targets sales ◎% increase, ◎% profit margin achieved」 — **specific figures required**
4. **Implementation Structure** (0.5 pages): Who does what, external partners (construction firms, designers, etc.) names and certifications

**Adoption Rate Enhancement Tips**:
- Insert 3–5 graphs/charts (improve numerical visibility)
- Rigorously apply 「current challenge + solution + expected effect」 three-step structure
- Avoid unsupported fantasy targets (「2x sales」 「3x profit」) completely lacking credibility

Common Chinese pitfall: 「First-time Japanese business plan writing」 results in machine-translation-level Japanese post-Chinese composition. Minimal **professional Japanese expression review** investment (several thousand yen) yields substantial value.

### Q4: Prior to Adoption Notification, Can Equipment Be Purchased in Advance?

**A:** **Principle: NO. Procurement and payment should occur only after adoption notification receipt.**
Exception scenarios permitting advance purchases:
- Adoption notification specifically states 「retroactive eligible period: from application date」, permitting post-application purchases in limited circumstances
- However, this remains system-dependent and inconsistent

**Critical Point**: Document preservation becomes mandatory — all receipts, invoices, delivery documents must be retained for subsequent submission. Purchasing without adoption authorization risks complete subsidy forfeiture if project expenses deviate from approved plan.

### Q5: If Application Is Rejected, Can Reapplication Occur Next Cycle?

**A:** **Yes, but rejection feedback becomes critical.**
- Rejection letters specify 「reason for non-adoption」 (document deficiency, insufficient planning, etc.)
- **Mandatory review**: Identify rejection cause; correct it thoroughly in reapplication
- **Timing consideration**: Most systems offer 2–3 recruitment cycles annually; certain systems wait 6–12 months before reapplication eligibility
- **Practical advice**: Consult specialists after rejection to diagnose deficiencies before resubmission

Chinese proprietors often abandon subsidy pursuit after single rejection, missing next-cycle opportunities. Persistence with corrections yields success rates significantly above initial attempt.

### Q6: What Role Do Specialists (Tax Accountants, Labor Consultants, etc.) Play? Is Professional Support Essential?

**A:** **Professional support substantially improves adoption rate and reduces error risk.**
Specialist roles:
- **Tax Accountant (Zeirishi)**: Accounting record validation, financial statement review, post-subsidy tax treatment guidance
- **Labor Consultant (Sharoshi)**: Payroll documentation, social insurance verification, human resource grant applications
- **SME Management Consultant**: Business plan development, competitive analysis, numerical target validation
- **Gyoseishoshi (Administrative Procedures Specialist)**: Document preparation, application filing, revision request response coordination

**Cost consideration**:
- Flat-fee consultation: 50,000–150,000 yen
- Success-fee model (no fee if rejected, percentage of subsidy if approved): 10–15% of subsidy amount
- **ROI Analysis**: For subsidy amounts exceeding 1 million yen, professional investment typically yields 3–5x return through adoption rate improvement

For Chinese proprietors unfamiliar with Japanese application procedures and language nuances, professional support is practically indispensable. Attempting self-application without support carries substantial rejection risk.

## Chinese Proprietors' Specific Challenges and Solutions — How to Navigate Unique Obstacles

### Challenge 1: Visa Status and Residency Complications

**Issue**: Many Chinese proprietors operate on various visa statuses (Temporary Visitor to Japan visa, Business Manager visa, etc.); residency status instability creates subsidy ineligibility or retroactive refund risk.

**Solution**:
1. **Pre-application verification**: Confirm visa validity until application approval + implementation completion (minimum 18–24 months total)
2. **Document preservation**: Maintain recent Residence Card photocopies, immigration approval letters, visa extension documents
3. **Address alignment**: Ensure Residence Card, tax registration, business license, and bank account registration all reflect identical address
4. If visa renewal anticipated during subsidy period, notify relevant supervising authority in advance

### Challenge 2: Accounting Records in Insufficient Japanese Format

**Issue**: Many Chinese proprietors maintain dual records (Chinese and Japanese), incomplete Japanese records, or non-standardized systems; Japanese subsidy authorities require strict double-entry bookkeeping per Japanese standards.

**Solution**:
1. Implement standardized Japanese accounting software (Yayoi Accounting, Freee, etc.) minimum 6 months prior to application
2. Hire provisional accounting support (part-time Zeirishi) for record cleanup and PDF export
3. Maintain original receipts, invoices, payment documentation systematically; photograph and organize chronologically
4. If records have gaps, create supplementary documentation with supporting evidence before application

### Challenge 3: Limited Japanese Business Writing Capability

**Issue**: Business plan documents, application statements, and revision responses require clear, professional Japanese. Machine translation or grammatically awkward Japanese significantly reduces reviewer confidence.

**Solution**:
1. Draft business plan in English or Chinese first
2. Engage professional translator (Japanese translation agencies charge 100–200 yen per character; typical business plan ≈ 10,000–15,000 characters = 1–3 million yen worth of professional translation)
3. Alternative: Hire part-time Japanese business consultant (20,000–50,000 yen for thorough review and correction)
4. Prioritize clarity over eloquence — simple, specific sentences outperform complex, flowery language

### Challenge 4: Cultural Misalignment in Application Strategy

**Issue**: Chinese business culture emphasizes relationship networks (guanxi), flexibility, negotiation; Japanese subsidy systems prioritize objective criteria, documentation completeness, regulation adherence. Chinese proprietors often attempt 「personal connection」 strategies that backfire in bureaucratic contexts.

**Solution**:
1. **Accept bureaucratic process**: Japanese government processes deliberately minimize personal discretion; appeals to relationship or special circumstance lack efficacy
2. **Documentation rigor**: Japanese systems value comprehensive documentation over verbal explanation; invest time in written evidence and supporting materials
3. **Timeline patience**: Japanese decision-making emphasizes deliberation; rushing supervisors or requesting expedited processing creates negative impressions
4. **Regulatory compliance**: Strictly adhere to all reporting requirements, documentation deadlines, and procedural steps; Japanese authorities heavily penalize non-compliance

### Challenge 5: Misaligned Expectations Regarding Support Agencies

**Issue**: Chinese proprietors sometimes conflate consulting services with subsidy guarantee; firms offering 「guaranteed approval」 or 「no-fee-if-rejected」 models sometimes exploit this misunderstanding.

**Solution**:
1. **Realistic expectations**: No legitimate agency guarantees subsidy approval; authenticity requires conditional language (「subject to reviewing authority's decision」)
2. **Red flag detection**: Avoid agencies promising 「certain approval」, demanding upfront fees before application, or guaranteeing specific subsidy amounts
3. **Verified credentials**: Confirm consulting firm holds official 「Certified Support Agency」 (認定支援機関) designation from government
4. **Transparent fee structure**: Legitimate firms disclose complete fee schedules; success-fee models should specify percentage range and calculation basis
