## Fundamentals of GビズID and Its Functions

### What is GビズID (gBizID)?

GビズID is an **official government electronic application account system** jointly launched by Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications. Think of it simply as: **a digital ID card for businesses from the government**.

Beginning in 2020, the Japanese government made it clear: to apply for any government subsidy, financing support, certification, or license, you **must first register for GビズID**. As of March 2024, **over 1.5 million businesses** have registered and activated this account, covering manufacturing, services, construction, agriculture, and virtually all other industries across Japan.

**Core functions of GビズID include:**
- One-time registration, lifetime access (valid indefinitely, completely free)
- Cross-ministry subsidy applications spanning all central government agencies, prefectures, and municipalities (including subsidies from the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare for employment, METI's Monozukuri Subsidy, MEXT research grants, etc.)
- Electronic signature and identity verification capabilities
- Real-time tracking of application status

In short: **no GビズID = unable to apply for any subsidy, no matter how favorable the terms**. This is not optional guidance; it is a mandatory prerequisite.

## GビズID Registration Process and Timeline

### Step-by-Step Registration Guide (Total time: 2–3 weeks)

**Phase 1: Preparation (2–3 days)**
- Gather basic company information: corporate registry abstract (or individual number card), representative ID (passport/residence card), company email address
- Visit the official portal: [GビズID Portal](https://gbiz-id.go.jp/) (the only official Japanese-language entry point)
- Register for 仮登録 (temporary account), entering company name, representative name, and corporate number (for corporate entities)

**Phase 2: Authentication (3–7 days)**
- Government sends confirmation email to your corporate email address; click the verification link
- If selecting the "登記識別情報" (registration identification information) method (recommended), upload photos of your corporate registry abstract or individual number card
- The system automatically cross-references your information with Japan's Legal Affairs Bureau registration database to verify legitimate business status
- Approval typically takes **3–5 business days**; if no issues are found, verification passes

**Phase 3: Full Registration (1 day)**
- Receive 「本登録完了通知」(Full Registration Completion Notice) email
- Click the link in the email to set your own **ユーザーID (username) and パスワード (password)**
- Log in to the GビズID Portal dashboard and confirm that your company information is synced
- **From this moment onward, your account is ready for all subsidy online applications**

**Total time: 2–3 weeks (fastest scenario: 10 business days if information is complete and processed immediately)**

## Five Critical Points for GビズID and Subsidy Applications

### Core Rules for Electronic Subsidy Application

**Point 1: GビズID ownership = Corporate representative**
- Registration must use the **actual company representative's name** (for corporations, the representative director's name)
- Subordinate employee accounts do not have subsidy application authority; applicants must be the company representative or an authorized agent (with power of attorney)
- **Common mistake:** An employee registering under their own name creates a GビズID ineligible for subsidy applications; the company must re-register under the representative's name, wasting 1–2 weeks

**Point 2: Email address (corporate email) is critical**
- All GビズID notifications, subsidy approval results, and payment notifications are sent to the registered email address
- Strongly recommend using a dedicated corporate email address (e.g., info@xxx.co.jp), **avoid using the representative's personal mobile email**
- If the email address changes, subsidy approval notifications may be missed, and the company may be deemed to have "withdrawn," forfeiting subsidy eligibility
- **Essential setup:** Email auto-forwarding or multiple address registration to ensure no critical notifications are missed

**Point 3: Corporate number (corporate individual number) dual verification**
- At registration: corporate number is entered → system cross-references with Ministry of Justice database
- At subsidy application: re-confirmed
- If corporate registry information differs from registered GビズID information (company name change, address change, etc.), the subsidy application will be rejected
- **Regular check:** Every April, obtain a corporate registry abstract and verify alignment with GビズID registration data

**Point 4: Multi-company operators require separate registrations**
- If Company A's representative also wants to apply for Company B's subsidies, **B must have its own independent registration**
- One email address cannot be used for multiple legal entities
- Even for group companies, each entity requires one GビズID (sharing is prohibited)

**Point 5: Password management and periodic updates**
- After GビズID registration, **change your password every 90 days** (per security policy)
- If ID and password are compromised → third party applies for subsidies without authorization → risk of illegal subsidy receipt
- **Recommendation:** Use a password manager like 1Password + regular backups

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: I'm a sole proprietor (個人事業主). Can I register for GビズID? How much subsidy can I receive?

**A:** Absolutely. Sole proprietors have equal registration rights as corporations with no restrictions. Required documents: (1) individual number card or passport, (2) copy of business startup notification filed with the tax office. Eligible subsidies for sole proprietors include the Sustainability Subsidy for Small Businesses (max 500,000 JPY), Work Style Reform Support Program (max 6 million JPY), and startup support subsidies (max 2 million JPY). According to 2024 statistics, sole proprietors average 500,000 to 2 million JPY annually in subsidies (most apply for a single small-business subsidy). Full registration to subsidy application can be completed within 1–2 weeks.

---

### Q2: My company was just established 2 months ago. Can I register for GビズID and apply for subsidies?

**A:** Yes, provided you meet the "within 3 years of startup" requirement (mandatory for most startup-focused subsidies). GビズID registration itself has no startup timeline restrictions and can be completed on the same day. However, at subsidy application time, you must submit: (1) corporate registry abstract, (2) financial statements for the past 2 periods or trial balance (for newly established companies: monthly cash flow records from establishment to present), (3) business plan. **Note:** If transitioning from sole proprietorship to incorporation, prior sole proprietorship performance may be evaluated alongside corporate records. Early-stage businesses typically receive slightly higher subsidy rates (usually 50% vs. 67% for new startups). Approval rates for first-year companies run 10–15% lower than established firms, but are far from impossible.

---

### Q3: Can GビズID registration and subsidy application happen simultaneously? What's the fastest timeline to begin applying?

**A:** No, they cannot happen simultaneously. **GビズID full registration (official activation) requires 3–5 business days**. Until then, subsidy application portals will not open; attempting to access will produce errors. Precise timeline: (1) temporary registration (same day) → (2) email verification + document review (3–5 days) → (3) full registration completion (1 day) = 3–6 business days total. In the fastest scenario, registering temporarily on Monday allows you to begin serious subsidy applications by Friday or the following Tuesday. **Strategy:** Check the next subsidy competition deadline in advance (typically every 2–3 months) and complete GビズID registration 3–4 weeks before the deadline.

---

### Q4: Is GビズID registration free? Are there annual renewal fees?

**A:** Completely free. Registration fee, annual membership fee, renewal fee—all zero. It's an official government service, so there are no costs to enterprises. GビズID **validity is indefinite** (once you complete full registration, you can use it for as long as your company exists). However, applying for subsidies may incur **actual expenses** in other areas: (1) consultation fees with Certified Support Agencies (free consultation windows available), (2) professional document preparation fees from Sharoshi (社会保険労務士 / social insurance and labor consultants) or Zeirishi (税理士 / tax accountants)—optional, (3) subsidy application agency fees (typically 5–15% of subsidy amount if approved, optional). The minimum-investment approach to completing GビズID registration through subsidy submission can be entirely cost-free.

---

### Q5: Can I apply for multiple subsidies simultaneously through GビズID? If multiple subsidies are approved in the same fiscal year, are there restrictions on receiving multiple payments?

**A:** Simultaneous applications are permitted. One GビズID allows you to apply for multiple subsidy programs at the same time (3–5 simultaneous applications are typical in practice). **However, restrictions apply after approval:** the general rule is that "duplicate payment of multiple subsidies for the same enterprise's identical expenses is prohibited." In other words, you cannot receive double payment for the same equipment purchase from both the Monozukuri Subsidy and the Business Restructuring Subsidy. However, **multiple approvals are OK for different expense categories** (e.g., receiving equipment investment funds from Monozukuri Subsidy and personnel costs from Work Style Reform Subsidy is permissible). The 2024 approval data shows that the average approved business secures **1.8 subsidies per year** (mix of single and multiple approvals). With strategic expense categorization, annual combined subsidies of 3–10 million JPY are entirely achievable.

### Q6: What if I forget my GビズID password or can no longer access the registered email address?

**A:** The GビズID Portal login screen has a "Reset Password" link. A reset email will be sent to your registered address, enabling a password reset within 24 hours. **However, if registered email access becomes completely unavailable** (e.g., corporate email was deleted), additional steps are needed: (1) submit a report via the GビズID Portal "Contact Us" form, (2) upload your corporate representative ID and corporate registry abstract, (3) within a few business days, email change is completed. During this period, subsidy applications are temporarily suspended. **Prevention strategy:** At GビズID registration, register (1) primary corporate email + (2) representative's personal email as backup accounts to prevent this scenario.

**Preparation Item 1: Financial statement and monthly trial balance preparation (1–2 weeks)**

**Preparation Item 2: Business plan and detailed subsidy project description (2–4 weeks)**

- **Solution:** Leverage free consultation from Certified Support Agencies (chambers of commerce, SME management consultants; multiple visits available), or pay a subsidy consulting firm 5,000–10,000 JPY for an initial draft review
