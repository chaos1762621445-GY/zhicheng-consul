# Osaka Prefecture & Osaka City Subsidy Inventory: Which Local Subsidies Can Chinese Business Owners in Japan Apply For?

According to 2023 data from Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare, Osaka Prefecture has approximately 275,000 SMEs (13% of the national total), with talent shortages exceeding 230,000 in manufacturing, food service, and tourism sectors. Due to this massive industrial gap, the central and local governments invest over 300 billion JPY annually in SME and entrepreneurship support subsidies. **Osaka Prefecture and Osaka City, as the economic centers of the Kansai region, have allocated approximately 48 billion JPY in total local subsidy budgets for fiscal 2024**. Chinese business owners who understand application strategies can simultaneously secure approval for 2–4 subsidies in their first year, accumulating 2–6 million JPY in funding support. This guide maps out the mainstream subsidy programs, specific amounts, eligibility requirements, and timelines available to Chinese entrepreneurs in the Osaka region, helping you understand at once which funds you can access.

## Osaka Prefecture SME Management Innovation Support Subsidy: The First Choice for Digital Transformation & Innovation Upgrades

The Osaka Prefecture Management Innovation Subsidy targets SMEs implementing management innovation strategies (including digital transformation, business process reform, and new product development). Managed by the Osaka Prefecture Department of Commerce, Labour and Employment, it recruits twice yearly in spring and autumn.

**Subsidy Amount & Subsidy Rate**: The eligible expense range typically covers 1–30 million JPY, with a subsidy rate of 1/2 to 2/3 (i.e., if you invest 1 million JPY, the government subsidizes 1–1.5 million JPY). Startups and certified agricultural-commercial cooperation enterprises receive bonus points and may qualify for a 3/4 subsidy rate. The vast majority of SME owners' first subsidy falls in the **1.5–4.5 million JPY range**.

**Eligibility & Target Criteria**:
- SMEs registered in Osaka Prefecture (including sole proprietors)
- Company established for 3+ years (startups may qualify for exceptions starting at 1 year)
- Must formulate a 「Management Innovation Plan」 (経営革新計画書) and obtain approval from the Osaka Prefecture SME Support Center or Chamber of Commerce
- **Foreign-operated enterprises receive equal treatment**—nationality is not a barrier, but the representative must hold a valid residence status and have completed tax registration in Japan for 1+ year

**Eligible Expense Categories**:
Equipment & machinery purchases (up to 20 million JPY maximum), system construction costs (IT adoption and system development qualify), branding & marketing expenses, training & human resource development. **Critical: Personnel expenses, taxes, loan interest, and other costs are strictly excluded from eligible expenses**—before applying, have a professional help you reclassify your budget.

**Application Process & Timeline**:
1. Mid-May: Spring recruitment announcement (typically accepts applications mid-May through end-June)
2. Mid-July: Expert review & selection decision (approximately 1.5 months review period)
3. September: Subsidy allocation decision and contract signing
4. September–following March: Project execution period (typically 6 months)
5. Following May: Submission of completion report and final inspection
6. June–July of following year: Full subsidy disbursement

**Common Pitfalls for Chinese Entrepreneurs**:
- **Misconception 1**: Thinking you can submit an application in Chinese or mixed Chinese-English—officials require Japanese-language submission, necessitating Japanese proficiency at test level or hiring a Japanese-language secretary.
- **Misconception 2**: Overstating sales growth targets—reviewers reference industry average growth rates (typically 8–15%) to assess feasibility; targets deviating from reality result in direct rejection.
- **Bonus Points**: If your plan involves SDG sustainability objectives (e.g., environmental protection, health industry, regional revitalization), review scores receive significant bonuses.

## Osaka City Manufacturing, Commerce & Service Competitiveness Enhancement Subsidy: Strengthened Support for Manufacturing + Service Industries

The Osaka City government has established a dedicated subsidy program for manufacturing, commerce, and service businesses within the city, with larger subsidy scales and high industry inclusiveness for Chinese enterprises (restaurants, tourism, cross-border e-commerce, logistics, etc. are all eligible).

**Subsidy Amount & Subsidy Rate**: Eligible expenses range from 1–50 million JPY, with a subsidy rate typically 1/2 (strategically important sectors rarely reach 2/3). **Most businesses receive actual amounts in the 2–8 million JPY range**, averaging 20–30% higher than prefecture-level subsidies. This is the main subsidy for Chinese restaurants, retail, and logistics enterprises.

**Application Target & Eligibility Requirements**:
- SMEs registered and actively operating in Osaka City (capital ≤300 million JPY or employees ≤1,000)
- No sales requirements for the past 2 years, but must satisfy either 「5+ employees in Osaka」 or 「2+ years of business continuity」
- **Special Note for Chinese Enterprises**: If your company primarily serves Chinese markets or China-Japan trade (e.g., cross-border e-commerce, import-export, Chinese restaurant chains), qualifying as an 「emerging industry category」 may increase the subsidy rate to 2/3

**Eligible Expense Categories**:
- Equipment & machinery purchases (production equipment, POS systems, cooking equipment, etc.)
- Building modifications & interior construction (store renovations, office upgrades)
- Software & system development costs
- New product development & service planning expenses
- Sales promotion & marketing (advertising, Little Red Book / TikTok promotion and other online marketing costs can be included—this is a benefit)

**Application Process & Standard Timeline**:
- Usually rolling applications by quarter: Q1 (January–February), Q2 (April–May), Q3 (July–August), Q4 (October–November)
- Results announced within approximately 60–90 days after each submission deadline
- After selection, sign contract and receive advance payment (typically 50% of total subsidy); project execution period 2–6 months
- After completion, submit proof materials; upon passing final inspection, receive remaining payment

**High-Frequency Scenarios for Chinese Entrepreneurs**:
Mr. Liu operates a China-Japan cross-border e-commerce company in Osaka City. In October 2023, he simultaneously applied for 3 subsidy programs: the Osaka City Manufacturing Subsidy (automatic packaging machine + WMS system, applying for 6 million JPY), Employment Incentive Grant (hiring 5 Chinese employees), and Digital Support Subsidy (ERP system). After 8 months of application and review, he received approval for approximately 9.8 million JPY in June 2024, covering 60–70% of first-half equipment investment, significantly alleviating cash flow pressure. This is a classic 「multi-track application strategy」—not simultaneous submission across all programs, but strategic staggered applications after understanding each subsidy's recruitment window.

## Employment Adjustment & Employment Incentive Grants: High-Efficiency Subsidies for Employee Recruitment & Training

Osaka Prefecture and Osaka City have established dedicated subsidies for new hires, employee training, and layoff adjustments. These are particularly important for rapidly expanding Chinese enterprises.

**Subsidy Types & Amounts**:

**① Employment Incentive Grant (New Hire Category)**
- Target: New hires in Osaka Prefecture aged 「35+」 or 「employment-disadvantaged persons」 (long-term unemployed, persons with disabilities, etc.)
- Subsidy Amount: 100,000–150,000 JPY per person monthly; subsidy period typically 12 months
- **Practical Significance for Chinese Enterprises**: If you hire skilled talent recruited from China (e.g., blue-collar workers, skilled technicians) and establish employment contracts and tax registration, qualifying individuals can receive 100,000–120,000 JPY per person monthly; annual subsidy per person reaches 1.2–1.8 million JPY. If you hire 3–5 people annually, total annual subsidy can reach 3.6–9 million JPY
- Application Cycle: Approval within approximately 30 days; subsidy begins the following month and continues with monthly reporting

**② Human Resource Development Grant (人材育成事業助成金)**
- Purpose: Company provides vocational training, language training, and qualification exam support expenses for new employees
- Subsidy Amount: Up to 500,000–1 million JPY per person annually
- Subsidy Rate: 3/4 (i.e., if you spend 40,000 JPY on training, receive 30,000 JPY subsidy)
- **Chinese Enterprise Case**: You provide recruited Chinese employees with Japanese-language training (annual cost 300,000 JPY) and qualification exam fees (100,000 JPY), totaling 400,000 JPY to receive 300,000 JPY subsidy—nearly full coverage of training costs

**Eligibility & Requirements**:
- Enterprises with registered operations and complete social insurance registration in Osaka Prefecture/City
- Employment contracts must satisfy 「6+ months」 「30+ hours weekly」 and other compliance requirements
- Applicants must submit subsidy application within 60 days of hire (hard deadline; late submissions forfeit benefits)

**Application Process & Key Timelines**:
- Company first hires employee → submits application form to supervising Hello Work (public employment office)
- Review period approximately 15–30 days
- Subsidies use 「reimbursement」 model: submit attendance records and payslips at month-end; disbursement arrives mid-following month

**Common Misconceptions & Bonus Points**:
- **Misconception**: Thinking subsidy arrives the month of hire—actually uses reimbursement model; fastest receipt is the following month. **Startups must reserve adequate cash flow to bridge this time gap**.
- **Bonus Point**: If the hired employee was referred by Hello Work as a registered unemployed person, subsidy rate and amount optimize further; some regions reach 150,000 JPY+ monthly.

## Osaka Prefecture Regional SME Support Fund: Flexible Subsidies for Startups & Business Succession

For Chinese entrepreneurs launching new businesses or undergoing business succession (second-generation takeover or M&A succession), the Osaka Prefecture Support Fund is a specially accommodating window. Unlike standard subsidies, this fund employs a 「subsidy + financing」 combination model, highly friendly to cash-strapped startups.

**Subsidy Amount & Financing Combination**:
- Subsidy Portion: 500,000–5 million JPY (general subsidy rate 50%; i.e., you contribute half, government subsidizes half)
- Financing Portion: Additional unsecured or low-interest loan availability of 1–20 million JPY
- **Practical Case**: A startup restaurant investing 4 million JPY to open receives 2 million JPY subsidy + 1.5 million JPY loan through this fund; self-investment required only 500,000 JPY, dramatically reducing initial financing pressure

**Application Targets**:
- Entrepreneurs (including Chinese entrepreneurs in Japan; requires feasible business plans)
- Business successors (retiring owner; son/external manager takes over)
- Business restructurers (firms affected by pandemic, economic hardship seeking revival plans)
- **Special Provision**: Startup hiring persons with disabilities or elderly workers receive additional bonus points

**Financing Features**:
- Loan term extends up to 10 years with 5-year grace period (first 5 years pay interest only; principal repayment begins year 6)
- Interest Rate: Based on applicant credit rating, typically 0.6–2.0% (far below commercial bank rates of 3–5%)
- **Friendly Provision for Chinese Enterprises**: Even without 3 years of credit history in Japan, full approval remains possible if business plans are sufficiently detailed and guarantor arrangements are reasonable

**Application Process**:
- Submit business plan (事業計画書) to Osaka Prefecture Department of Commerce, Labour and Employment or local Chamber of Commerce
- Initial subsidy application review (approximately 45–60 days)
- Parallel financing application (can approve subsidy first, then supplement financing application)
- Subsidy and financing sign contracts simultaneously; pay 50% of subsidy as startup capital; first financing disbursement arrives

**Timeline Reference**:
- Application submission → First Review: 30–45 days
- Subsidy Selection Decision → Financing Approval: Additional 30–45 days
- Full Process from Application to First Funding Arrival: Approximately 90–120 days (20–30% faster than standard subsidies)

As Japan's government advances its DX (Digital Transformation) strategy, support subsidies for SME digitalization expand annually. Osaka Prefecture and Osaka City invested approximately 1.2 billion JPY in this sector in fiscal 2024—the highest ever.

**Subsidy Target & Amount**:
- Subsidy Range: 500,000–30 million JPY
- Subsidy Rate: **1/2–2/3** (higher than other subsidies)
- Typical Subsidy Amount: **1.5–6 million JPY** (average amount received by SMEs)
- Target Enterprises: All sectors' SMEs (including retail, food service, logistics, manufacturing, etc.)

**Eligible Expenses**:
- Cloud System Adoption (SaaS, ERP, CRM and other cloud services annual fees or initial construction costs)
- E-commerce platform construction (independent sites, multi-channel sales systems)
- Business Management Systems & POS upgrades
- Payment System Implementation (Alipay, WeChat Pay and other multi-currency collection systems)
- Data Analytics & BI deployment

**High-Frequency Applications for Chinese Enterprises**:
- Restaurant Chains: POS + membership management system implementation can apply for 2–4 million JPY
- Retail / Resale Enterprises: Independent e-commerce site + inventory management system can apply for 1.5–3.5 million JPY
- Import-Export Trading Enterprises: ERP system and international logistics tracking integration can apply for 3–6 million JPY
- Logistics / Delivery Enterprises: Fleet management systems, IoT monitoring can apply for 5–10 million JPY

**Simplified Eligibility Requirements**:
- Registered and actively operating enterprises in Osaka Prefecture/City
- Employee count ≤300 (basic requirement)
- Corresponding IT vendor must be 「DX Support Service Provider Certified」 enterprise (Japan-registered qualified system vendor)—meaning your selected development company must appear on the official list. **Many Chinese outsourcing teams lack qualifications; must use domestic system vendors with Japan certifications**

**Application Process & Timeline**:
- Spring Window (May–June), Autumn Window (September–October) recruit in two rounds
- Application submission + IT vendor evaluation: approximately 30–45 days review
- After selection, contract with vendor and launch project
- Project execution typically 3–6 months
- Submit completion report and invoices after finishing; upon approval, receive disbursement

**Key Risk Alert**:
- IT Vendor Credential Verification Failure = Subsidy application rejected outright; therefore **must confirm IT vendor's registration on Osaka Prefecture/City certified list before applying**
- Many Chinese entrepreneurs seek cheaper Chinese development teams, but if the team lacks Japan corporate registration or certified credentials, cannot qualify as legitimate supplier for application

## Employment Maintenance Grant (雇用調整助成金): Cushion Subsidy for Post-Pandemic Recovery & Personnel Adjustments

Although this subsidy's peak period has passed (2020–2022 pandemic era), Osaka Prefecture maintains it in 2024 as a fallback tool for 「distressed industry relief」. Particularly helpful for import-export or food service enterprises affected by international conditions and trade policy fluctuations.

**Subsidy Content & Amount**:
- Purpose: When enterprises face operational difficulties requiring temporary closure or reduced working hours, government directly subsidizes a portion of employee wages during that period
- Subsidy Rate: **Principle 2/3** (i.e., if employee monthly salary 300,000 JPY, government subsidizes 200,000 JPY; enterprise covers 100,000 JPY)
- Subsidy Period: Maximum 6 months per round; can apply for extension; cumulative maximum 12 months
- Monthly Subsidy Cap: Per-employee monthly subsidy 8,355 JPY × working days (typically 40,000–50,000 JPY monthly per employee)

**Application Requirements**:
- Average sales for past 3 months down 20%+ compared to same period previous year
- Enterprise has clear 「Business Recovery Plan」 indicating recovery within 6–12 months
- Employees must maintain employment relationships (no layoffs)

**Chinese Enterprise Case**: An import-export trading company's sales plummeted 30% in first half of 2024 due to trade policy changes. By reducing work hours (6 hours daily → 4 hours) while retaining 25 employees, monthly subsidy reached approximately 1.25 million JPY (25 people × 50,000 JPY), sustaining operations for 6 months through crisis.

**Application Cycle**:
- Document submission → Approval approximately 20–30 days
- Subsidy uses 「reimbursement」 model; report monthly, receive disbursement mid-following month
- Must reserve 1–2 months cash flow buffer period

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: I am a Chinese business owner; my company registered only 1 year ago. Can I apply for Osaka Prefecture subsidies?

**A:** Yes, but options are limited. Per Japan's Management Innovation Subsidy rules, enterprises typically require 3+ years establishment; however, startup exceptions allow 1–2 year enterprises to apply if established with clear innovation plans and approved by Chamber of Commerce. More friendly options include 「Osaka Prefecture Regional Support Fund」 (specifically targets entrepreneurs; no 3-year limit) and 「Manufacturing, Commerce & Service Subsidy」 (relatively relaxed requirements—only need 5+ employees or 2+ years operation). **Recommend prioritizing startup-friendly subsidies; avoid directly competing for 3-year-threshold programs**.

### Q2: My company mainly does China-Japan trade; employees are primarily Chinese. Will this affect subsidy applications?

**A:** No. Japan's subsidy policies make no nationality distinctions; as long as your company has formal corporate registration in Osaka Prefecture/City, completes tax registration, and maintains social insurance compliance, you have equal application rights. Employee nationality also doesn't affect this—your hired Chinese, Vietnamese, and other employees, once employment contracts and social insurance registration complete, even become bonus conditions for 「Employment Incentive Grant」. Instead, cross-border trading enterprises, qualifying as 「emerging industry category」 or 「regional revitalization」, sometimes receive upward subsidy rate adjustments (50% → 66%). **Single Key Point**: All application documents and reports must be completed in proper Japanese; recommend hiring Japanese-language secretaries or professional institutions for assistance.

### Q3: How long from subsidy application to receiving funds? My enterprise urgently needs capital; how can I get it fastest?

**A:** Standard subsidy applications typically require **4–6 months** from submission to first funding arrival. Specific to Osaka's mainstream programs:
- Management Innovation Subsidy: 7–9 months application-to-selection-to-execution-to-disbursement
- Manufacturing Subsidy: Similar cycle, 5–8 months
- Digital Support Subsidy: Faster, 4–6 months
- Employment-related Subsidies: Fastest, 2–3 months application-to-approval-to-disbursement
  
If urgently needing cash, recommend **combination application strategy**: simultaneously submit 2–3 different subsidy applications, staggering by review speed; for instance, prioritize Employment Incentive Grants (2–3 months to receive) as short-term cash flow, parallelly apply for large equipment subsidies (4–6 months)—two subsidies' funding can relay solve your working capital needs.

### Q4: My application was rejected; can I appeal or reapply?

**A:** Yes. After initial rejection, you have right to request 「審査結果異議申し立て」 (Examination Results Objection) process—formal review channel requiring submission within 30 days of rejection notice. **Pass rate approximately 15–25%**, depending on whether first review indicated genuine deviations. Should review also fail, no further appeal exists, but **next year can reapply after revising business plan per feedback**. Recommend consulting professionals immediately after rejection to identify failure causes (typically insufficient plan, unreasonable financial budget, low feasibility score) rather than blindly pursuing review.

### Q5: I applied for subsidy but haven't received approval yet; can I advance-fund and start the project now?

**A:** **Absolutely not**. Japan's subsidy system has strict rule: 「補助対象経費は採択決定後に発生したものに限る」—only expenses incurred after subsidy selection decision qualifies for subsidy coverage. If you self-fund equipment purchases or payments before selection, these expenses cannot be listed as subsidy-eligible, essentially resulting in your loss. The sole exception—some subsidies like the Support Fund allow 「expenses incurred pre-application to be retrospectively subsidized」—requires prior written authorization from supervising authority; otherwise treated as violation. **Recommend delaying large investments until subsidy selection notification arrives.**

### Q6: I simultaneously applied for 3 Osaka subsidies; all approved. Can subsidy totals offset each other? Or can I receive all separately?

**A:** This is a common minefield. **Principle is 「同一経費不得重复補助」 (same expense cannot receive duplicate subsidy)**—if you purchase one 1-million-JPY equipment, applying both Manufacturing Subsidy (50% subsidy) and Digital Support Subsidy (50% subsidy), supervising authority will detect duplication, demanding 「補助金の調整」 (subsidy adjustment)—requiring return of excess subsidies; potential violation liability follows. **Correct approach: at planning stage, clarify 「対象経費」 (target expenses) distinction**—for example, Equipment A uses Manufacturing Subsidy, System B uses Digital Support Subsidy; different expense items map to different subsidy sources, allowing compliant receipt of multiple subsidies. Strongly recommend pre-application consultation with professionals to create 「補助金マップ」 (subsidy map) planning entire year's subsidy strategy, clarifying expense allocation among subsidies, avoiding post-hoc adjustments or refunds.

## Three Major Pain Points for Chinese Entrepreneurs Applying for Subsidies & Solutions

**Pain Point 1: Japanese Document Preparation Too Complex**  
Business plans, eligible expense schedules, funding plans and other documents require professional Japanese meeting official format standards; many Chinese owners struggling independently risk format errors and logical inconsistencies, facing direct rejection. **Solution**: No need to write entirely yourself; Osaka Chamber of Commerce and local SME Support Centers provide free business plan writing support (can designate Chinese-speaking instructors); typically 2–3 consultations complete the draft with minimal cost.

**Pain Point 2: Uncertain How Much Subsidy You Qualify For**  
Many owners lack numbers before applying, uncertain what their investment scale, industry type, and employee composition qualify for, leading to excessive or insufficient investment. **Solution**: Before formal application, spend 30 minutes completing a simple 「Self-Check Worksheet」 (company scale, industry, investment items, employee count)—instantly rough-estimate eligible subsidy categories and amount ranges (typically ±10–20% accuracy).

**Pain Point 3: Project Launch Before Funds Arrive; Cash Flow Breaks**  
Many enterprises can't wait for subsidies, self-funding first; when subsidy arrives, already unable to fund next-phase investment. **Solution**: Recommend 「subsidy + financing combination」 strategy—simultaneously apply for subsidies and commercial short-term loans or policy financing as bridge funding (Osaka offers many commercial loans at 0.6–2% annual interest; repay immediately upon subsidy arrival). Maintains momentum without wasting subsidy windows.
