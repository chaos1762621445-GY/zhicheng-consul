# What Is a Certified Support Agency, and Why Do You Need One to Apply for a Japanese Subsidy?

If you are a Chinese business owner in Japan and have ever considered applying for a Japanese startup subsidy, business-expansion subsidy, or management-innovation subsidy, you have surely spotted one key term among the eligibility requirements: the "Certified Support Agency." This unfamiliar-sounding phrase is in fact the essential key that unlocks the door to Japan's government subsidies.

Many small and medium-sized business owners miss out on millions of yen in subsidy funding simply because they do not understand the role of a Certified Support Agency. This article takes a practical, hands-on look at the system to help you understand why working with a Certified Support Agency is indispensable when applying for a subsidy.

## What Is a Certified Support Agency: The Basics

### The Official Definition and Its Essence

A Certified Support Agency (認定経営革新等支援機関), formally the "Certified Management Innovation Support Agency," is an organization officially certified by Japan's Small and Medium Enterprise Agency as having the professional capacity to provide management support to small and medium-sized enterprises. These organizations include:

- **Tax accountant (Zeirishi) firms** (comparable to tax-agent firms in China)
- **Certified public accountant firms** (financial audit organizations)
- **Chambers of Commerce and Industry** (similar to a federation of industry and commerce)
- **Societies of Commerce and Industry** (regional SME commerce associations)
- **The management support divisions of shinkin banks and other banks**
- **Professional management consulting firms**

As of 2024, there are more than 40,000 Certified Support Agencies across Japan. This figure reflects an important fact: **the supply of Certified Support Agencies is ample**, so your company can absolutely find a suitable one.

### How a Certified Support Agency Differs from an Ordinary Consulting Firm

You may ask: aren't there consulting firms everywhere in Japan? Why must the agency be "certified"?

The crucial difference here is **legal effect**. Under the Act on Promotion of the Productivity Revolution (プロダクティビティ向上特別措置法), only a certificate issued by a support agency officially certified by the Small and Medium Enterprise Agency is recognized under Japan's government subsidy system.

To put it another way: if applying for a subsidy is like taking an exam, then the Certified Support Agency is the officially recognized "proctor." Their opinions and recommendations directly influence how reviewers evaluate your company. A consulting firm that has not been certified—no matter how detailed a business plan it writes for you—cannot satisfy the legal requirement.

## Why a Certified Support Agency Is Mandatory When Applying for a Subsidy

### A Hard Legal Requirement

Japan's major subsidies—the **Small Business Sustainability Subsidy** (小規模事業者持続化補助金), the **Business Expansion Subsidy** (事業拡大補助金), and the **Management Innovation Subsidy** (経営革新補助金)—all clearly stipulate in their eligibility conditions:

**The applicant company must undergo management consultation with a Certified Support Agency or collaborate with it on formulating the subsidized-project plan.**

This is not a suggestion but a hard requirement. Without support from a Certified Support Agency, the application form will be rejected by the system before it is even submitted.

Specifically, the 2024 Small Business Sustainability Subsidy (up to 1.5 million yen) requires applicants to receive management guidance from a Certified Support Agency at least once within three months. The application form has a dedicated section for the support agency to fill in its assessment; without content in that section, the entire application is invalid.

### Recognition and Credibility

An opinion letter issued by a Certified Support Agency amounts to a "third-party endorsement." When reviewers see support opinions from an officially certified organization, they are more inclined to trust the feasibility of your business plan.

According to statistics from the Small and Medium Enterprise Agency, **subsidy applicants who receive support from a Certified Support Agency typically have an approval rate 30–50% higher than those who do not.** This figure comes from officially published adoption-rate statistics.

Take the Small Business Sustainability Subsidy as an example:
- In 2023 the overall applicant adoption rate was about 40%
- But among applicants who worked closely with a Certified Support Agency, the adoption rate was as high as 60–70%

This means the involvement of a Certified Support Agency directly raises your probability of successfully obtaining a subsidy.

### A Guarantee of Business Plan Quality

Certified Support Agencies are certified precisely because they must pass management-related qualification examinations and command professional knowledge such as financial analysis, market research, and strategic planning.

When you work with a Certified Support Agency, they help you:

1. **Diagnose the current situation** – Analyze your financial data, market position, and competitive advantages in depth
2. **Identify problems** – Point out the bottlenecks in your current operations
3. **Develop strategy** – Help set realistic business goals and growth paths
4. **Refine the plan** – Ensure the business plan is logically rigorous and backed by verifiable data

A strong business plan lets reviewers understand within a few minutes what your company is, what the problem is, what the solution is, and what results to expect. This clarity of presentation directly affects your score.

## How to Find the Right Certified Support Agency

### Search Methods and Channels

Japan's Small and Medium Enterprise Agency maintains a complete database of Certified Support Agencies, which you can search in the following ways:

**Official search website:** Log in to the "経営革新等支援機関検索システム" (Certified Management Innovation Support Agency Search System) at: https://www.mirasapo-plus.go.jp/

The system lets you search by:
- Prefecture
- Type of support (finance, IT, market development, etc.)
- Supported language (English, Chinese, etc.)

For example, if your company is in Tokyo, you can enter "東京都 中文" (Tokyo, Chinese) to find certified agencies that support Chinese-owned businesses.

**Other channels:**
- **Chambers of Commerce and Industry** – Call the Chamber of Commerce and Industry in your area directly, and they will recommend local Certified Support Agencies
- **Bank of Japan / shinkin banks** – Many financial institutions have Certified Support Agencies in-house; simply ask
- **Chinese Chambers of Commerce** – Chinese business organizations in Japan usually partner with professional Certified Support Agencies

### Selection Criteria and Recommendations

Once you have a few candidate Certified Support Agencies, how do you choose? We suggest evaluating them from the following angles:

**1. Professional qualification background**
- Are they a tax accountant (Zeirishi) or a certified public accountant?
- Or is the support provided through an organization such as a Chamber of Commerce and Industry?

Generally speaking, a tax accountant or accounting firm with individual qualifications may offer deeper financial-analysis support.

**2. Understanding of your industry**
- Have they previously served companies in your industry?
- Do they understand the trends and competitive landscape of that industry?

For example, if you run a restaurant business, a Certified Support Agency that has helped many food-service companies apply for subsidies will give you a higher success rate.

**3. Language ability**
- Can they communicate with you in Chinese or Japanese?
- Do they understand the operational characteristics of Chinese-owned businesses?

This is especially important. Although the application documents in Japan must be in Japanese, being able to plan and communicate in a language you are comfortable with will greatly improve efficiency.

**4. Scope of service and fees**
- How much is the support fee? (Usually between 20,000 and 100,000 yen)
- How long will the support take?
- How many face-to-face meetings are included in the process?

A healthy engagement should include at least three to five formal face-to-face consultations, not just email exchanges.

**5. Reference cases and success rate**
- Ask to see cases of companies they have previously helped get approved
- Ask about the subsidy adoption rate of the companies they support
- Find out whether they can assist with the later execution and reporting of the subsidy

## The Practical Process of Working with a Certified Support Agency

### The Complete Steps from Consultation to Engagement

**Step 1: Initial consultation (Weeks 1–2)**
- Contact the Certified Support Agency and explain your company's basic situation and your intention to apply for a subsidy
- Most organizations offer a free initial consultation (usually one hour)
- In this consultation you should ask: how they will help you, how much it will cost, and how long it will take

**Step 2: Signing the support agreement (Weeks 2–3)**
- If both sides reach agreement, sign a "Management Innovation Support Agreement"
- The agreement clearly sets out the support content, timeline, and fees
- This agreement must be submitted when applying for the subsidy

**Step 3: Company diagnosis and information gathering (Weeks 3–6)**
- The support agency conducts an in-depth diagnosis of your company
- You need to provide financial statements (past two to three years), staffing details, product/service information, market conditions, etc.
- The support agency may also conduct an on-site visit to observe your actual business operations

**Step 4: Formulating the business plan (Weeks 6–10)**
- Based on the diagnosis, the support agency helps you develop the business plan
- The business plan usually includes: current-situation analysis, problem diagnosis, solutions, specific measures, financial projections, etc.
- You and the support agency go through multiple rounds of discussion and revision

**Step 5: Compiling the subsidy application documents (Weeks 10–12)**
- Under the support agency's guidance, complete the remaining parts of the subsidy application
- The support agency reviews all materials to ensure logical consistency and accurate data
- The support agency signs and stamps the application

**Step 6: Submission and follow-up (Weeks 12–14)**
- Electronically submit the application to the subsidy operating body
- The support agency usually helps with follow-up and answers any questions the reviewers may have

From Step 1 to Step 6 usually takes three to four months. **This is exactly why experts advise you to start contacting a Certified Support Agency at the very latest four months before the subsidy application deadline.**

### Cost and Return on Investment

The cost of working with a Certified Support Agency is usually:
- Support fee: 20,000–100,000 yen (average 50,000 yen)
- Your time cost: preparing materials, participating in consultations, etc.

By comparison, the scale of the subsidies obtained:
- Small Business Sustainability Subsidy: 500,000–1.5 million yen
- Business Expansion Subsidy: 1 million–5 million yen
- Management Innovation Subsidy: 5 million–20 million yen

**The return on investment (ROI) is extremely high.** Even with only a 50% success probability, the average expected return can reach more than 100 times the support fee.

## Points of Special Note for Chinese Business Owners

### Language and Cultural Differences

As a Chinese business owner, you may encounter the following particular challenges when working with a Certified Support Agency:

**1. The logic of expression in a Japanese business plan**
The way a Japanese business plan presents its logic differs greatly from Chinese. Japanese tends toward:
- More structured paragraphing
- More frequent data support
- Explicit statements of "cause and effect" and "preconditions"

A good Certified Support Agency understands these differences and will not simply have you "translate" a Chinese plan; instead, it helps you reorganize the content in a Japanese mode of thinking.

**2. Consistency of financial statements**
Many Chinese business owners may be used to keeping one set of books for tax filing in China and another for investor reporting. But in Japan, the tax office and the subsidy reviewers cross-verify your financial data, which must be entirely consistent.

The Certified Support Agency helps you ensure the completeness and authenticity of all financial figures.

**3. Expressing your management philosophy**
A Chinese management philosophy is often a statement of virtues such as "benevolence, righteousness, propriety, wisdom, and trust," but Japan's subsidy reviewers focus more on the "social significance of the business" and "quantifiable goals."

A good Certified Support Agency helps you translate abstract philosophy into specific, measurable business goals.

### Recommended Types of Support Agencies

For Chinese business owners, the following types of Certified Support Agencies may be a better fit:

1. **Tax accountant firms with Chinese-language services** – You can communicate directly in Chinese, avoiding misunderstandings
2. **Chambers of Commerce and Industry that have served many Chinese-owned businesses** – Experienced with the characteristics of Chinese-owned businesses
3. **Support divisions of financial institutions in cities with large Chinese communities such as Tokyo and Osaka** – Geographically convenient and culturally attuned

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: My company is very small (only three employees). Can I still find a Certified Support Agency?

**A:** Absolutely. In fact, the clients Certified Support Agencies serve are precisely small and medium-sized enterprises, including micro-enterprises and sole proprietors. Japan's subsidy system was designed to support the growth of small businesses in the first place. Whether you are a sole proprietor with just one employee or a small company of 50, you can find a Certified Support Agency willing to support you. In the search system, you can filter by "for sole proprietors" or "for micro-enterprises" and see a large number of professional support organizations aimed at small businesses. Many Chambers of Commerce and Industry offer free or low-cost preliminary consultation specifically for sole proprietors.

### Q2: Can I skip working with a Certified Support Agency and apply for the subsidy directly?

**A:** Legally, no. Under the subsidy eligibility conditions, an application lacking the support-opinion letter from a Certified Support Agency will be judged as "not meeting the application requirements" at the preliminary screening stage and rejected outright. In other words, you cannot even enter the formal review stage. This is not an industry convention but a legal requirement. In the past, some business owners tried to bypass this requirement, only to see weeks of application preparation go to waste. So working with a Certified Support Agency is not optional but mandatory. This rule also exists to ensure that subsidies are used for genuinely strategic, professionally assessed business improvements.

### Q3: I already have a tax accountant. Do I still need to find a Certified Support Agency?

**A:** Yes, but you already have a big advantage. If your tax accountant holds "Certified Support Agency" certification, then they can serve as the Certified Support Agency at the same time. In that case you do not need to find a separate organization, and communication costs are lowest. If your current tax accountant does not have this certification, you have two options: first, ask them to apply to the Small and Medium Enterprise Agency for certification (though this is fairly cumbersome); second, find another certified support agency to collaborate with your tax accountant. In many cases, tax accountants and Certified Support Agencies work in tandem—the tax accountant handles the financial-analysis portion, and the Certified Support Agency handles the strategy and market analysis. Such a combination often produces higher-quality application documents.

### Q4: Can the Certified Support Agency's service fee be deducted from the subsidy?

**A:** No. The Certified Support Agency's service fee does not fall within the scope of "subsidized project" expenses, so even if you successfully obtain the subsidy, you cannot use it to pay the support agency's fee. This fee is your company's own investment cost. In other words, the 20,000–100,000 yen support fee comes out of your own pocket. But from an ROI perspective, it is a very worthwhile investment. If the subsidy is 1.5 million yen and the support fee is 50,000 yen, the ROI is 30 times. Moreover, if your application is rejected due to insufficient quality, that 50,000-yen investment could cost you a 1.5 million-yen subsidy. So the support fee should be viewed as an "insurance premium" rather than a "cost."

### Q5: Might a support agency carelessly rush my application just to earn its fee, causing problems to surface in later review?

**A:** This is a reasonable concern, but the system has safeguards built in. First, the Certified Support Agency's certification is granted by the Small and Medium Enterprise Agency; if irresponsible support leads to falsified or misleading application data, the organization faces the serious consequence of having its certification revoked. This penalty is very heavy, so most Certified Support Agencies take every application seriously. Second, after a subsidy is obtained there is an "assessment period" (usually three to five years) during which the government reviews how the subsidy is used. If false reporting is discovered, the company must return the subsidy and may face fines, and the support agency is also held jointly responsible. Therefore, a responsible Certified Support Agency will in fact scrutinize your data and plan more strictly—preferring to have you revise multiple times rather than let false content pass.
